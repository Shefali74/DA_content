# Leave and Work From Home Policy

## Work From Home Policy

### Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their probation period (90 days) are eligible for work-from-home arrangements.

### Guidelines
- Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week
- WFH days must be pre-approved by your direct manager via the HR Portal
- Core hours (10:00 AM - 4:00 PM local time) must be maintained regardless of location
- Employees must be reachable via Teams/Slack during core hours
- A stable internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps) is required
- WFH is not a substitute for childcare or eldercare arrangements

### Approval Process
1. Submit WFH request on HR Portal at least 48 hours in advance
2. Manager approves/rejects within 24 hours
3. Recurring WFH schedules can be set up for the quarter

## Annual Leave

### Entitlement
- 0-2 years tenure: 18 days per year
- 2-5 years tenure: 22 days per year
- 5+ years tenure: 28 days per year
- Carry forward: Maximum 5 days to next calendar year

### Sick Leave
- 12 days per year (non-cumulative)
- Medical certificate required for 3+ consecutive days
- Submit via HR Portal within 48 hours of return

### Public Holidays
- Follow the regional holiday calendar published in January each year
- Floating holidays: 2 per year (can be used for any occasion)

## Reimbursement for WFH Equipment
- One-time WFH setup allowance: Up to $500 (requires receipts)
- Monthly internet allowance: $50/month for approved WFH employees
- Submit claims through Expense Portal with manager approval
