# Hardware Provisioning and Laptop Policy

## New Laptop Request

### Eligibility
- New hires (laptop provisioned during onboarding)
- Replacement cycle: Every 3 years from issue date
- Damaged/stolen devices (requires incident report)

### Available Models
| Role | Standard Option | Premium Option |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| Developer/Engineer | MacBook Pro 14" M3 / ThinkPad X1 Carbon | MacBook Pro 16" M3 Max |
| Business/Operations | MacBook Air M3 / ThinkPad T14 | MacBook Pro 14" M3 |
| Data Science/ML | MacBook Pro 16" M3 Max | Custom workstation (approval required) |

### Request Process
1. Log into IT Service Portal at https://itportal.internal/hardware
2. Select "New Device Request" from the catalog
3. Choose your preferred model (within your role allocation)
4. Submit justification if requesting premium tier
5. Manager approval required (auto-routed)
6. IT procurement processes within 5-7 business days
7. Device setup and configuration: 1-2 business days after receipt
8. Collect from IT desk (Floor 3, Room 312) or request shipping for remote employees

### Laptop Return Policy
- All devices must be returned within 5 business days of last working day
- Data wipe is performed by IT before any reassignment
- Failure to return results in payroll deduction of device value

## Peripheral Equipment
- Monitor (up to 27" 4K): Available through IT catalog
- Keyboard/Mouse: Standard issue during onboarding
- Headset: Provided for roles requiring frequent calls
- Additional peripherals: Submit request with manager approval

## Repairs and Maintenance
- Hardware issues: Raise ticket at itportal.internal/support
- Standard repair SLA: 3 business days
- Loaner device available during repairs (subject to availability)
- Accidental damage: Covered under corporate warranty (first incident free)
